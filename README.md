I'm an aspiring **MERN Stack Web & App Developer** and an **AI Enthusiast** driven by a passion for building structured, dynamic, and intelligent digital experiences. 

Currently pursuing my BS in Economics and Finance at the University of Karachi, I am also actively advancing my technical expertise as a Front-End Development Intern at DevelopersHub Corporation.
